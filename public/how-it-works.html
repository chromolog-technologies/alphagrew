How it works

From institution setup to placement outcomes, in eight stages.

AlphaGrew follows the order a placement season actually runs in. Each stage builds on data the previous one captured, so nobody types the same thing twice.

End to end

The placement season on AlphaGrew

  1. 01Institution onboarding and setup
  2. 02Student and faculty onboarding
  3. 03Profile and resume preparation
  4. 04Opportunities and eligibility checks
  5. 05Preparation and AI interview practice
  6. 06Placement drive coordination
  7. 07Applications and outcomes
  8. 08Reporting and continuous improvement

Your flow depends on your setup

The stages below describe the full product. Which steps you see depends on the features switched on for your institution and how it is configured, and some features are still in development or planned, as marked.
  1. 1

    Institution admin

    Institution onboarding and setup

    Add departments, courses and academic years, assign roles, and set the placement policies your institution follows.

    • Departments, courses and academic yearsIn current build
    • Roles and permissionsIn current build
    • Placement policiesIn current build
  2. 2

    Institution admin and staff

    Student and faculty onboarding

    Invite placement officers and faculty by email. Students register and the institution approves each registration, or brings students in from existing records.

    • Staff invitations and student registrationIn current build
    • Bulk importPlanned
  3. 3

    Students

    Profile and resume preparation

    Students complete their career profile with projects, skills and experience, upload a resume and get feedback on how to improve it.

    • Student career profileIn current build
    • Resume uploadIn current build
    • Resume analysisIn development
    More about Career Intelligence
  4. 4

    Placement officer and students

    Opportunities and eligibility checks

    The placement cell adds companies and roles with their criteria. Each student's record is checked against those criteria, so eligible lists are built rather than typed.

    • Companies, drives and applicationsIn current build
    • Job eligibility checksIn current build
    • Job matchingPlanned
  5. 5

    Students, faculty and trainers

    Preparation and AI interview practice

    Students practise role-specific mock interviews as often as they need and read the feedback. Faculty see where their classes need help.

    • AI mock interviews (text)In current build
    • Interview feedback reportsIn development
    • AssessmentsPlanned
    More about AI Interviewer
  6. 6

    Placement officer

    Placement drive coordination

    Publish the drive, notify the students it applies to, and manage each round: aptitude, technical and HR, through to results.

    • Companies, drives and applicationsIn current build
    • AnnouncementsIn current build
    • NotificationsIn current build
    • Rounds, shortlists and offersIn current build
    More about Placement Management
  7. 7

    Students and placement officer

    Applications and outcomes

    Students apply in one place and follow each round. Offers and placement records are captured, and the institution's offer policy is checked automatically.

    • Companies, drives and applicationsIn current build
    • Placement policiesIn current build
    • Rounds, shortlists and offersIn current build
  8. 8

    Leadership and placement cell

    Reporting and continuous improvement

    Review placements by department and readiness by batch, see which skills the next batch is missing, and plan training before the next season.

    • Placement reportsIn development
    • Readiness scoreIn development
    • Batch skill-gap insightsPlanned

Getting started

What a pilot looks like

A proposed rollout. Timings depend on your data and academic calendar, and are agreed at kick-off.

  1. Phase 1

    Kick-off

    Agree scope, departments and the batches in the pilot, and the features to switch on.

    With: Principal or director, placement officer

  2. Phase 2

    Data setup

    Set up the academic structure and bring in students and faculty, then check the data together.

    With: Office staff, heads of department

  3. Phase 3

    Training

    Short sessions for admins, the placement cell, faculty and student coordinators.

    With: All roles

  4. Phase 4

    First drive

    Run a real or practice drive end to end on AlphaGrew.

    With: Placement officer, students

  5. Phase 5

    Review

    Look at usage and outcomes together and decide the next phase.

    With: Leadership, placement officer
