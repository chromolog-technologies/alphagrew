Placement Management In current build

Run the placement season from one shared record.

Companies, jobs, eligibility, applications, schedules, notices, rounds, outcomes and reports, in the order a placement cell works through them. Each institution sets its own rules, and each role sees only its part.

Book a demoSee the process

Why it helps

Less copying between spreadsheets, more time with students

  • 01

    One record per drive

    Company, job, dates and who may apply live in one place the whole placement cell can see.

  • 02

    Rules from records

    Eligibility is meant to be read from academic records, not retyped into a spreadsheet for every company.

  • 03

    Students apply themselves

    Students see open drives and apply in one step, and can follow their own applications.

  • 04

    Reports already counted

    Placement figures by drive and batch come from the same records, not a separate tally.

What's real today

  • Jobs, eligibility rules, applications, rounds, offers, placement policy checks, announcements and email notifications are in the current build.
  • Placement reports are in development; downloadable report files are not built yet.
  • There is no SMS, and no integration with job portals, applicant tracking systems or ERPs.

The process

Nine stages from first contact to final report

Each stage shows who does it and how far it is built. Your institution's set-up decides which stages you use.

  1. In current build

    Placement officer

    Company and job details

    Company, role, package, drive date and the departments the drive is open to.

  2. In current build

    Placement officer

    Eligibility rules

    CGPA, backlogs, attendance, department, batch, school scores, skills and profile completion, combined for each job.

  3. In current build

    AlphaGrew

    Eligible student list

    The students who meet every rule, built from live records, with the reason for each rule that fails.

  4. In current build

    Student

    Applications

    Students apply to open jobs. Rules, deadlines and the placement policy are checked when they apply.

  5. In current build

    Placement officer

    Drive schedule

    Each round has its own date, and students are notified when it is scheduled.

  6. In current build

    Placement officer

    Notices and notifications

    Announcements to departments or batches, plus in-app and email notifications for jobs, rounds and offers. SMS is planned.

  7. In current build

    Placement officer

    Interview stages

    Aptitude, group discussion, technical and HR rounds, with shortlists per round.

  8. In current build

    Placement officer

    Outcomes

    Results for each round, offers with deadlines and final placement records.

  9. In development

    Institution

    Reports

    Drive statistics and results by batch are in development. Downloadable report files are not built yet.

Plain limits

What we do not promise yet

  • No automated messaging. Nothing is emailed or texted to students today.
  • No third-party integrations. No job portal, applicant tracking or ERP connections.
  • No concurrency guarantees. Repeat applications are ignored, but behaviour under heavy simultaneous use has not been load-tested.
  • No automatic selection. Shortlists and offers are decided by people at the institution and the company.

Questions

About Placement Management

More in the full FAQs.

Can we set our own eligibility rules?

Yes. Each job can combine rules on CGPA, backlogs, attendance, department, batch, school scores, skills and profile completion. They are checked against student records rather than typed in, and students see which rule they missed.

Does AlphaGrew email or text students automatically?

Email, yes: students get in-app and email notifications for new jobs, rounds, application updates and offers, and can choose which they receive. SMS is planned, not built.

Can a student apply twice by mistake?

Applying again to the same drive does not create a second application. We have not load-tested simultaneous requests, so we do not make guarantees about them.

Does it connect to job portals, ATS or our ERP?

Not today. There are no integrations with job portals, applicant tracking systems or ERPs. Bulk import from spreadsheets is planned.

Can recruiters log in?

Not yet. Recruiters work through the placement cell. A recruiter portal and shared candidate profiles are planned. See For recruiters.

Does a readiness score affect eligibility?

No. Eligibility comes only from the criteria a company and the institution set. Readiness and interview scores are for preparation.
