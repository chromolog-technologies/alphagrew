Career Intelligence In development

See the distance between today and the role a student wants.

Career Intelligence brings a student's profile, academic record, resume and mock interviews together, compares them with what a role needs, and turns the difference into practice steps. It guides preparation; it does not predict placement.

Book a demoHow readiness is calculated

Why it helps

Preparation guided by evidence

  • 01

    Evidence over claims

    A skill shown in an interview counts for more than one only listed on a resume.

  • 02

    Gaps named early

    Students see what a target role needs and what they have not shown yet, well before the drive.

  • 03

    A plan, not a number

    Every gap comes with practice steps a student can work through and mark as done.

  • 04

    Effort aimed well

    Placement and training staff can put their time where students need it most.

What's real today

  • The readiness indicator, skill-gap view and practice suggestions are in development, built on the student profile and mock interviews.
  • Assessments are not built, so they are not counted yet. Opportunity fit and batch skill gaps are planned.
  • Readiness and skill gaps are rule-based calculations, not AI predictions.

Skill gaps In development

Each competency, checked against real evidence

For a target role such as backend or frontend developer, AlphaGrew lists the competencies the role needs and looks for the strongest evidence of each one.

  • A mock interview score counts first, then an assessment result.
  • A skill only listed on a resume counts for half and is marked unverified until shown.
  • When the practice is for a posted job, that job's required skills are added.

Readiness, explained

How the readiness indicator is calculated

Every part and weight is visible. The weights are a product design choice and have not been validated against placement outcomes.

Parts of the readiness indicator and their weights
PartWeightWhere it comes from
Resume20%Latest resume analysis
Academics25%CGPA on a 100-point scale, less 10 for each active backlog
Assessments25%Not built yet, so currently left out
AI interviews30%Recent mock interview scores

A part with no data is left out and the other weights are scaled up to fill the gap, so a missing assessment never counts as zero.

Opportunity fit Planned

Why a job suits a student, in plain words

Planned: for each posted job, show which required skills a student has already shown and which they still need, so preparation goes to the right drives.

Fit is guidance only. It never decides who may apply; eligibility rules do.

Responsible use

Support for development, not a verdict

  • Not a prediction. Readiness has not been validated as a predictor of placement or job performance, and should not be read as one.
  • No guaranteed outcomes. Insights help students prepare. They do not promise an interview, an offer or a salary.
  • Never an eligibility blocker. Who may apply is set by the company and the institution, not by a readiness score.
  • Visible to the student. Students see their own insights and can ask for the underlying record to be corrected. See the Privacy Policy.

Questions

About Career Intelligence

More in the full FAQs.

Does the readiness score predict who will be placed?

No. It is a coaching indicator. Its weights are set by product design, not derived from placement outcomes, and it has not been validated as a predictor of employment. A high score does not guarantee an offer, and a low one does not rule one out.

Can the score stop a student from applying to a drive?

No. Readiness is never an eligibility rule. Whether a student may apply depends only on the criteria the company and the institution set. See Placement Management.

What happens when there is no data for a part of the score?

That part is left out and the remaining weights are rescaled, rather than counted as zero. Assessments are not built yet, so today the indicator uses resume, academics and interviews.

Is AI used to make these insights?

The skill-gap view and the readiness indicator are calculated by fixed rules, so the same evidence gives the same result. AI is used for resume parsing and interview evaluation when a provider is configured. Either way, the insights support a student's development and are not a guarantee of any employment outcome.

Who can see a student’s insights?

The student, and staff at the student's institution whose role allows it. Insights are not shared with recruiters.
