Draft for legal review

This document is a starting draft written with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the DPDP Rules in mind. It is not legal advice. A qualified lawyer must review it, and every highlighted placeholder must be filled in, before it is published.

1. Who we are

AlphaGrew is a product of Chromolog Technologies, [legal entity name and registered office address] (“AlphaGrew”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains how we handle personal data when you use the AlphaGrew platform, this website and related services.

2. Our role and your institution's role

Most personal data on AlphaGrew is provided by, or on behalf of, an educational institution that uses the platform. For that data, the institution is the Data Fiduciary and decides why and how the data is processed. AlphaGrew acts as a Data Processor and processes it only on the institution's instructions under a written agreement.

For data we collect for our own purposes, such as demo requests and account security logs, AlphaGrew is the Data Fiduciary.

3. Personal data we process

  • Identity and contact: name, email, phone number, institution, role, enrolment or employee number.
  • Academic records: department, course, batch, attendance, marks, results and backlogs, as provided by the institution.
  • Career data: resumes, skills, documents, AI mock interview transcripts and feedback, applications and placement results.
  • Recruiter data: names and contact details of company representatives, job descriptions and drive details.
  • Technical data: device and browser information, IP address, sign-in times and security logs.
  • Website enquiries: the details you send us when you request a demo.

4. Why we process it

  • To provide the platform to your institution: records, mock interviews, career insights and placement drives.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse and keep each institution's data separate.
  • To respond to demo requests and support queries.
  • To improve the service using aggregated or de-identified information.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use student data for advertising.

5. Consent

Where consent is the basis for processing, it is collected through a clear notice in plain language, and you can withdraw it at any time as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect processing that happened before it.

6. Students under 18

Some students may be under 18. For them, the institution must obtain verifiable consent from a parent or lawful guardian before their personal data is processed on AlphaGrew. We do not track, profile or target advertising at children.

7. AI features

The AI Interviewer and Career Intelligence features process interview responses and resumes to generate practice feedback. These features:

  • run through our servers; AI provider keys are never exposed to your browser;
  • are used for practice and guidance only, not to make automated hiring, admission or academic decisions;
  • show students the feedback and scores generated about them.

We use third-party AI model providers as sub-processors: [name providers and confirm their data-use terms].

8. Who we share data with

  • Your institution and the staff it authorises, according to their roles.
  • Recruiters, only the candidate information the institution or the student chooses to share for a specific drive.
  • Sub-processors who help us run the service, such as hosting, email delivery and AI model providers: [list of sub-processors].
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

9. Where data is stored

Data is stored on cloud infrastructure in [hosting region]. If any data is processed outside India, we do so only as permitted under the DPDP Act.

10. How we protect data

We use reasonable security safeguards, including separation of each institution's data, role-based access, encryption in transit and access logs. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform the affected institution without delay so that the Data Protection Board of India and affected individuals can be notified as the law requires.

11. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as your institution's agreement specifies, and then delete it unless the law requires us to keep it: [retention periods by data type].

12. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you may:

  • get a summary of the personal data processed about you;
  • ask for your data to be corrected, completed, updated or erased;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity;
  • have your grievance addressed.

If your data was provided by your institution, contact the institution first; we will help it respond.

13. Grievance Officer

[Grievance Officer name, email and postal address]. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

14. Cookies and storage

The platform uses essential browser storage to keep you signed in securely. This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. To understand which pages are useful, the public website uses Vercel Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies and without identifying you; it is not used inside the signed-in platform.

15. Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. Material changes will be communicated to institutions.
