Platform and features

Everything a placement season needs, on one student record.

Seven feature areas that share the same data, so each step uses what the previous one learned. Every feature is labelled with where it stands today.

Placement operations

Run every drive from one list

The placement cell manages companies, drives and applications in one place, with institution policies applied the same way every time.

  • Companies, drives and applicationsIn current build

    Company, drive and job records with roles, packages and deadlines, and an application pipeline from applied to placed, with every status change recorded.

  • Placement policiesIn current build

    One-offer and dream-offer limits, offer deadlines, application caps, a minimum profile, opt-outs and debarment, checked automatically every time a student applies.

  • Rounds, shortlists and offersIn current build

    Aptitude, technical and HR rounds with schedules, shortlists, results per round, offers with deadlines and placement records.

Student profiles and resumes

One profile and resume per student, kept current

Students keep their own career profile and resume up to date, and get feedback on how to make them stronger.

  • Student career profileIn current build

    Projects, certifications, experience, skills and education in one profile the student keeps current.

  • Resume uploadIn current build

    PDF and Word resumes stored privately, with clear messages when a file cannot be read.

  • Resume analysisIn development

    AI feedback on resume quality and structure, with comparison against a job description planned.

  • Document verificationPlanned

    Students upload mark sheets and certificates; staff verify them once.

Explore Career Intelligence

Job opportunities and eligibility

Show each student the roles they qualify for

Company criteria are checked against the student's academic record, so eligible lists are built rather than typed, and students know why they do or don't qualify.

  • Job eligibility checksIn current build

    CGPA, backlog, attendance, department, batch, school-score, skill and profile rules evaluated against live student records, with the reason for every pass or fail.

  • Job matchingPlanned

    A match score and a personal feed of the roles that fit each student best.

Assessments and interview preparation

Practice that looks like the real thing

AI mock interviews ask role-specific questions and follow up on weak answers. Assessments will add aptitude and technical tests.

  • AI mock interviews (text)In current build

    Typed mock interviews that ask role-specific questions and follow up on weak answers.

  • Interview feedback reportsIn development

    Rubric scores with reasons and suggestions after each interview; staff review is planned.

  • AI mock interviews (voice)In development

    Server-side speech transcription exists but is not yet available in the interview screen. There is no video interview.

  • AssessmentsPlanned

    Aptitude and technical tests from a question bank, with results that feed shortlists.

Career readiness insights

See readiness for a student and for a batch

Readiness is built from evidence the platform already holds, so mentors and the placement cell can act before the season starts.

  • Readiness scoreIn development

    A coaching indicator per student built from resume, academic and interview evidence. Not a prediction of placement.

  • Batch skill-gap insightsPlanned

    Which skills most of a batch is missing, so training can be planned before the season.

Fits how your institution works

Your fields, your modules, your dashboards

Every institution runs placements a little differently. Add the fields you track, create modules for your own processes, build dashboards for each role and arrange the menu, all without code.

  • Custom fieldsIn current build

    Add the fields your institution tracks to students, staff, courses, exams and other records, without code.

  • Custom modulesIn current build

    Create modules of your own, such as hostel requests or internships, with their own records and fields.

  • Dashboard builderIn current build

    Build dashboards from charts and figures, and share them with the roles that need them.

  • Arrangeable navigationIn current build

    Administrators reorder and hide menu items so each role sees what it uses most.

Notifications and reporting

The right notice to the right students, and numbers already counted

Announcements reach a department, batch or the whole institution. Reports give leadership placement figures without rebuilding them by hand.

  • AnnouncementsIn current build

    Notices to a department, batch or the whole institution.

  • NotificationsIn current build

    In-app and email alerts for new jobs, rounds, application updates and offers, with per-person preferences. SMS is planned.

  • Placement reportsIn development

    Placement summaries by department today; exports for annual reports and accreditation planned.

  • Audit logIn current build

    An append-only record of who changed what, which administrators can search and filter.

Platform foundations

Built for institutions, from the ground up

Roles, records and data separation sit under every feature. Recruiters will get their own portal; today they work through the placement cell.

  • Roles and permissionsIn current build

    Institution admin, placement officer, faculty and student each see only what their role needs.

  • Staff invitations and student registrationIn current build

    Invite staff by email, let students register, and approve or reject each registration.

  • Departments, courses and academic yearsIn current build

    The academic structure that student records, batches and eligibility rules hang from.

  • Attendance, exams and marksIn development

    Marks entry, review and result publishing, with attendance and batch management being completed.

  • Multiple campusesIn current build

    Campuses and batches under one institution, with records kept to the right campus.

  • Institution data isolationIn current build

    Every record belongs to one institution, and every request is checked so it reaches only that institution's data.

  • Bulk importPlanned

    Bring in students and staff from spreadsheets, with an error report for rows that need fixing.

  • Recruiter portalPlanned

    Recruiters post roles and review shared candidates directly.
