What gets in the way

Challenges for recruiters

Every campus sends a different sheet

Candidate lists arrive in different formats, and checking them against your criteria takes time.

Coordinating rounds by email

Slots, venues and results move between your team and the placement cell in long threads.

Little visibility between stages

It is hard to see how many candidates are at each stage across campuses.

Capabilities

What AlphaGrew provides

The recruiter portal is planned. Today, recruiters work with the placement cell, which already runs eligibility checks, rounds and offers on AlphaGrew.

Recruiter sign-in is not available yet. AlphaGrew does not make hiring decisions or rank candidates for recruiters.
  • Job eligibility checksIn current build

    The placement cell enters your criteria, and they are checked against academic records the institution maintains.

  • Shared candidate profilesPlanned

    See only the profile sections a student has made visible, for the drive they applied to.

  • Rounds, shortlists and offersIn current build

    The placement cell schedules your rounds and records results and offers, so every candidate's stage is up to date.

  • Recruiter portalPlanned

    Follow candidate stages for each campus drive in your own view.

Workflow

How it works for you

  1. 1

    Share your criteria

    Send the role, package and eligibility criteria to the placement cell.

  2. 2

    Receive eligible candidates

    The institution's records decide who meets the criteria.

  3. 3

    Review authorised profiles

    See the profile sections each student has chosen to share for the drive.

  4. 4

    Run the drive and track stages

    Coordinate rounds with the placement cell and follow candidates to offer.

Benefits

What changes for recruiters

Consistent candidate information

Profiles follow one structure across campuses that use AlphaGrew.

Criteria checked against records

Eligibility is based on the institution's academic records rather than self-reported details.

Fewer coordination threads

Schedules and results are shared in one place with the placement cell.

Questions

Common questions

Access and privacy

As designed, recruiters will see only candidates who applied to their drive, and only the profile sections those students chose to share. Contact details will stay hidden until a candidate is shortlisted, and each recruiter view of a profile will be logged.

Can recruiters sign in today?
Not yet. The recruiter portal is planned. Today, recruiters work with each placement cell directly.
Does AlphaGrew screen or rank candidates?
No. Eligibility comes from your criteria and the institution's records. Mock interview scores are not shared with recruiters, and hiring decisions are yours.
