What gets in the way

Challenges for placement officers

Eligibility lists built by hand

Every company has different cut-offs, and each change means filtering spreadsheets again.

Notices get lost

Drive details go out through several groups, and students who should apply sometimes never see them.

Status chasing between rounds

Tracking who applied, who cleared which round and who already holds an offer takes hours each week.

Questions

Common questions

Access and privacy

Placement officers see the student information their role allows. Companies only receive what the institution chooses to share for a specific drive.

Are eligibility checks automatic?
Yes. Each job's rules are checked against student records, so the eligible list is built rather than typed, and students see which rule they missed.
Can we keep using our own offer policy?
Yes. Each institution sets its own rules, such as one-offer and dream-offer limits, and they are checked automatically when a student applies.
