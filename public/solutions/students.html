Challenges for students

Losing track after applying

Questions

Common questions

Access and privacy

You can see your own scores and feedback. Mock interview scores are for practice and are not shared with recruiters. Your institution decides which profile details are shared for a drive.

How do I get access?
Register through your institution, or ask your placement cell for an invitation. Your institution approves each registration.
Do mock interviews affect my placement?
No. They are for practice. Eligibility comes from the criteria set by the company and your institution.
Can I use it on my phone?
Yes. AlphaGrew is a web application that works in a mobile browser.
