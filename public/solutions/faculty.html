What gets in the way

Challenges for faculty

No clear view of who is prepared

Faculty often find out who struggled with interviews only when results come back.

Preparation sits outside academic work

Marks and attendance live in one place and placement preparation in another, so the full picture is hard to see.

Generic sessions for varied needs

Without knowing which skills a class lacks, training sessions end up covering everything for everyone.

Capabilities

What AlphaGrew provides

How faculty can take part, depending on their role.

Faculty access is set by each institution. Faculty views of student preparation are planned, not yet available.
  • Attendance, exams and marksIn development

    Enter and review marks for your classes, in the system placement eligibility will read from.

  • Faculty view of student preparationPlanned

    See your own classes' interview practice and resume progress.

  • AssessmentsPlanned

    Set aptitude and technical tests and review the results.

  • Mentor notes and follow-upsPlanned

    Record guidance for a student and follow up on agreed actions.

  • AnnouncementsIn current build

    Post notices to the departments and batches you are responsible for.

Workflow

How it works for you

  1. 1

    Get access from your institution

    Your administrator invites you and sets what your role can see.

  2. 2

    Keep academic records current

    Marks you enter become part of the student record used later in placement.

  3. 3

    Review preparation

    Check which students in your classes have practised and where they struggle.

  4. 4

    Act on it

    Plan sessions around common gaps and follow up with the students who need it.

Benefits

What changes for faculty

Time spent where it matters

Sessions can focus on the skills a class is missing, not a general syllabus.

One record for each student

Academic and preparation information about a student sits in one place.

Support before results

Students who need help can be reached before drives, not after rejections.

Questions

Common questions

Access and privacy

Faculty see only the classes and students their role covers. Interview practice scores are for preparation and are not shared with recruiters.

Will faculty see every student in the institution?
No. Each institution sets faculty access, normally limited to the classes or students a faculty member is responsible for.
Is there a faculty dashboard today?
Faculty roles and marks entry are part of the current work. A faculty view of student preparation is planned and not yet available.
