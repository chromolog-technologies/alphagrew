About us

Connecting how students prepare, how institutions coordinate and how recruiters hire.

AlphaGrew is a campus placement and career intelligence platform developed by Chromolog Technologies. We are building it so that preparation, and the placement process around it, reaches the whole batch.

The problem

Placement work is split across too many places

In many institutions, the information a placement season depends on is scattered. Each piece works on its own, but nothing connects them.

Records live in separate spreadsheets

Marks, backlogs and student details sit with different departments, so every eligibility list is rebuilt by hand.

Notices travel through message groups

Drive announcements, forms and deadlines are scattered, and students miss what applies to them.

Preparation reaches only a few

A small training team cannot give hundreds of final-year students the interview practice they need.

Recruiters get lists, not context

Companies receive spreadsheets of names, with little that shows who meets the criteria or who is prepared.

Our approach

One student record that connects three kinds of work

AlphaGrew links preparation, coordination and opportunity, so each step uses what the previous one already captured.

  1. Student preparation

    Profiles, resumes and AI interview practice that build readiness long before the first drive.

  2. Institutional coordination

    Academic records, eligibility and drive management for the placement cell, faculty and leadership.

  3. Recruitment opportunities

    Drives and candidate lists built from records the institution has verified.

Mission and vision

Mission

Give every student structured preparation and a fair shot at opportunity, and give institutions one connected system to run placements.

Vision

Institutions that move from simply managing students to continuously measuring, developing and improving their career readiness.

What we believe

Product principles

Students come first

Every feature should leave a student better prepared or better informed. Students see their own data and feedback.

AI assists, people decide

AI helps students practise and helps staff prioritise. It never makes a hiring, admission or academic decision.

The institution owns its data

Each institution's data is kept separate and processed only to provide the service.

Honest about what exists

We label every feature as in the current build, in development or planned, and never present a mockup as a live product.

Built for how campuses work

Semesters, departments, cut-offs, backlogs and offer policies are part of the product, not afterthoughts.

Evidence over impressions

Readiness comes from what students have actually done: their profile, resume and practice, not self-ratings alone.

Who builds it

AlphaGrew and Chromolog Technologies

AlphaGrew is a product developed by Chromolog Technologies. Chromolog Technologies designs, builds and maintains the platform; AlphaGrew is the name institutions, placement teams, students and recruiters see and use.

Registered company details, and the terms under which institutions use AlphaGrew, will be published in the Privacy Policy and Terms before launch.
