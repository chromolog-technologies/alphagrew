Working together

Every role adds to the same student record

What one role records is what the next one relies on, so nobody has to ask for the same information twice.

  1. 01

    Institutions

    Sets up departments, roles and placement policies.

  2. 02

    Placement Officers

    Brings in companies and runs each drive.

  3. 03

    Faculty

    Keeps academic records current and supports preparation.

  4. 04

    Students

    Builds a profile, practises and applies.

  5. 05

    Recruiters

    Shares criteria and hires through the placement cell.

Access and privacy

Each role sees only what it needs

  • Each institution's records are kept to that institution.
  • Administrators decide what placement officers and faculty can see and change.
  • Students see their own scores and feedback. Mock interview scores are not shared with recruiters.
  • Recruiters will see only candidates for their own drives, and only the profile sections students chose to share.
